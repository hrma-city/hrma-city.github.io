2 · F-01 / F-05 · 15 分钟案例讨论卡

项中文样品 F-01English sample F-05
标题一个 50 间的团队询价：接还是不接A 50-room group enquiry: accept or decline
适用第 15 讲前 15 分钟 / T、U 档Before Lesson 15 / Track T, U
情境平峰日，团队报 50 间、团价 650，餐饮净贡献 180/间；散客 ADR 1000、餐饮净贡献 200/间；变动成本 120；预测散客需求 180；总房量 200（教学简化场景）。Off-peak day. Group: 50 rooms at 650, F&B net contribution 180/room. Transient ADR 1000, F&B 200/room. Variable cost 120. Forecast transient demand 180. Capacity 200 (simplified teaching scenario).
问题 1分别算团队与散客的单间净贡献。为什么不能用纯房费收入比？Compute per-room net contribution for both segments. Why is room revenue alone insufficient?
问题 2真置换量该用 50 间，还是 MAX(0, 180−(200−50))？预测 OCC 60% 意味着什么？Is the true displacement 50 rooms, or MAX(0, 180−(200−50))? What does a 60% forecast occupancy imply?
问题 3若团队另有会议净收入 12,000，结论会不会翻转？写出你新增的假设。If the group brings 12,000 in meeting net revenue, does the decision flip? State your added assumption.
预期分歧多数学生会直接比 650 与 1000；教师引导到「净贡献 + 置换量 + 安全边际」三层。Most students compare 650 vs 1000 directly. Steer toward contribution, displacement, and safety margin.
教师提示不公布答案；先让两组各给一个结论，再让第三组指出对方漏了哪个口径。Do not reveal the answer. Have two groups commit, then a third identify the missing denominator.

1写出 RGI 的正确公式。若 MPI = 105.88、ARI = 90.88，RGI 是多少？Write the correct RGI formula. Given MPI 105.88 and ARI 90.88, what is RGI?
2净价 = 牌价 × (1 − 成本率)。牌价 620、成本率 16%，净价是多少？成本率要扣几次？Net rate = rate × (1 − cost rate). Rate 620, cost 16%: what is net rate, and how many times is cost deducted?
3Pickup 与 Pace 的区别是什么？哪一个是「窗口内净新增预订」？Distinguish Pickup from Pace. Which one is net new bookings within a window?

4 · F-03 / F-07 · 一张图讲清 RevPAR 与 GOPPAR

层中文视觉笔记 F-03English visual note F-07
第一层收入侧：RevPAR = ADR × OCC，只看客房、只看收入Revenue side: RevPAR = ADR × OCC. Rooms only, revenue only.
第二层全收入：TrevPAR 把餐饮、会议与其他收入按可售房夜摊到每间Total revenue: TrevPAR spreads F&B, events and other revenue over available room-nights.
第三层利润侧：GOPPAR 扣掉变动与运营成本；NRevPAR 再扣渠道成本Profit side: GOPPAR deducts operating costs; NRevPAR further deducts channel costs.
一句话RevPAR 是收入侧过程指标，不是经营结果；利润结果看净贡献 / GOPPAR。RevPAR is a revenue-side process metric, not the outcome. Judge profit via contribution / GOPPAR.
常见错把 RGI 当「终极成绩单」；把 RevPAR 上涨直接等同于赚钱Treating RGI as the final scorecard, or assuming RevPAR growth equals profit growth.
