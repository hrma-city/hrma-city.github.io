1. 一页纸产品说明 / One-Page Product Brief

EN (ready to copy):
RMC is a revenue-management teaching system for hotel management faculty and in-service revenue managers. It combines a 46-chapter textbook, 15 codex decision tables, three question banks (466 / 253 / 76 items) and a 2,673-file knowledge base. Every conclusion carries a source reference — chapter, table number, or case ID. A teacher can use it to run a full course; a property can use it to make a traceable daily pricing decision. We do not promise RevPAR uplift or certification outcomes; results depend on each property's own data.

中文对照：RMC 是面向酒店管理教师与在岗收益经理的收益管理教学体系。由 46 章教材、15 张法典判定表、三套题库（466 / 253 / 76 题）与 2,673 文件知识库构成。每条结论都带出处——章号、表号或案例编号。教师可据此开出完整课程，门店可据此做出可追溯的每日定价决策。我们不承诺 RevPAR 提升或认证通过，结果取决于本店自己的数据。
